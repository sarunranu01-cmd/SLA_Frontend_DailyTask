Sundar Pichai

Pichai Sundararajan (born June 10, 1972), known professionally as Sundar Pichai, is an Indian-American business executive. He serves as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Alphabet Inc. and its subsidiary, Google. Since stepping into this role, he has accelerated the company's focus toward Generative Artificial Intelligence, cloud infrastructure, and quantum computing breakthroughs.

Early Life:

  • Birth: He was born Pichai Sundararajan on June 10, 1972, in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India, into a Tamil Hindu family
  • Family Background: His mother, Lakshmi, worked as a stenographer, and his father, Regunatha Pichai, was an electrical engineer at GEC, a British conglomerate.

Education:

  • Schooling: He attended Jawahar Vidyalaya Senior Secondary School in Ashok Nagar, Chennai, and completed his Class XII at the Vana Vani school located at the IIT Madras campus.
  • Undergraduate Degree: He earned a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in metallurgical engineering from IIT Kharagpur in 1993.
  • Master's Degree: He holds a Master of Science (MS) in materials science and engineering from Stanford University School of Engineering.
  • Business Degree: He earned a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, where he was recognized as both a Siebel Scholar and a Palmer Scholar.

Career

Pichai worked in engineering and product management at Applied Materials and in management consulting at McKinsey & Company.[23] Pichai joined Google in 2004, where he led the product management and innovation efforts for a suite of Google's client software products, including Google Chrome[24] and ChromeOS, as well as being largely responsible for Google Drive. He went on to oversee the development of other applications such as Gmail and Google Maps.[25][26] On November 19, 2009, Pichai gave a demonstration of ChromeOS; the Chromebook was released for trial and testing in 2011, and released to the public in 2012.[27] On May 20, 2010, he announced the open-sourcing of the new video codec VP8 by Google and introduced the new video format, WebM.[28] Pichai served on Jive Software's board of directors from April 2011 to July 2013

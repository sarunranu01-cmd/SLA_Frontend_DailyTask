About

Established in 1959 with technical, academic, and financial assistance from the Government of West Germany, the Indian Institute of Technology Madras is a premier public institute of technology located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Officially recognized as an Institute of National Importance, the campus is a self-contained, beautifully wooded 250-hectare residential enclave carved out of the adjoining Guindy National Park, famously hosting diverse wildlife like blackbucks and spotted deer. Globally acclaimed for its excellence in technical education, basic and applied research, innovation, and industrial consultancy, IIT Madras has firmly secured its position as the top engineering institution in India, consistently ranking #1 in the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) engineering category year after year.

Zoho

About Company

Zoho is a global technology company that creates cloud-based software to help businesses manage their daily operations. Founded in 1996, Zoho offers more than 55 business applications for customer relationship management (CRM), accounting, human resources, email, project management, and collaboration. Its products are used by businesses of all sizes around the world. Zoho is known for providing affordable, secure, and easy-to-use software solutions that improve productivity and support business growth.

Services

Zoho offers a wide range of cloud-based business services, including Customer Relationship Management (Zoho CRM), accounting (Zoho Books), email and collaboration (Zoho Mail and Workplace), project management (Zoho Projects), human resource management (Zoho People), marketing automation, customer support (Zoho Desk), and business analytics. These services help organizations improve productivity, streamline operations, and manage their business efficiently.

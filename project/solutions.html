Rebuild 03

Rebuild lab · Future atlas

The job didn’t disappear. The job changed.

If automation reshapes IT work, the response is not to compete with AI at being AI. It is to direct, verify, govern, combine, and build.

01

Principle

Human + AI, with names on the work

Do not compete with AI at being AI. Learn to direct it. Verify it. Govern it. Combine it. Build with it.

Collaboration here means a pair: the model proposes; a person remains responsible. That is already how professional software is supposed to work when a compiler, a library, or a contractor is involved.

02

Pairs

Why humans remain in the loop

Generate ↔ Review

Drafts are cheap. Fit, security, and taste still need a critic.

Simulate ↔ Decide

Models can explore options. Someone still chooses under uncertainty.

Draft ↔ Own

Authorship in law and ethics is about who answers for harm.

Automate ↔ Govern

A workflow without logs and stop-rules is a hidden policy.

Predict ↔ Judge

Scores are not verdicts. Context, fairness, and exceptions are human.

Optimize ↔ Take responsibility

Speed that no one will defend in public is not an improvement.

Future skill DNA

04

Reskilling

Especially for students and first jobs

Continuous learning

Tools will move. Concepts (systems, security, evaluation) age slower than brand names.

Project-based evidence

A portfolio that shows review notes and failed attempts is more honest than a generated demo reel.

AI-assisted learning

Use models as tutors, then prove you can work without them in an oral or studio setting.

Domain + cross-disciplinary skill

Healthcare, energy, education, and finance errors are not generic. Pair computing with a field.

06

Education

If AI can generate answers, what should education teach?

Problem solving, critical thinking, verification, projects, communication, ethics, system understanding, real-world collaboration, and AI literacy. Assessment may need to capture process: briefs, critiques, orals, and studios—not only take-home files.

07

Firms

Company responsibility

Reskilling & mobility

Internal pathways beat discarding people who already know the domain.

Human oversight

Named owners, evals, and the right to stop a ship.

Responsible automation

Automate chores without deleting the junior ramp entirely.

Transparency & apprenticeships

Say when AI is in the loop. Keep entry-level seats as learning seats, not leftover tickets.

08

Public

Government and society — possible responses

Neutral options, not a manifesto: digital education, reskilling programmes, career transition support, public AI literacy, employment policy, social safety mechanisms, and access to tools. Which mix a country chooses is political; the exhibition only notes that labor shocks, if they occur, are not only private problems.
